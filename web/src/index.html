Every layer is placed in one world frame in micrometres (its own pitch and origin from its metadata) and resampled into the view: trilinear for grey and probability layers, nearest for masks and labels. Each layer picks its own pyramid level per view. Minimap: the active view's slice at a coarse level; click or drag it to move that view. The URL hash keeps the layers, their settings, the cursor and the zoom, so a copied link reopens the same view.
